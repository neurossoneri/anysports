Before a competition it is easy to jump ahead to the moment of the result. It helps to come back to the present action: what in your preparation is already done, what is today's task, and what can you control right now?

This is not a way to remove nerves instantly. It is a reason to look at your own approach to the start and to find support in concrete actions.
