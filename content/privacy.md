This notice explains what happens to personal data when you visit this website or write to me through it. It follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP, in force in its revised form since 1 September 2023) and its ordinance. I collect as little data as possible and use it only for the purposes named here.

You can use these details for any question about your data or to exercise your rights.

## What data I process, and why

**Visiting the website.** The servers that deliver this website process technical data such as your IP address, the date and time, the pages requested and your browser type. This is needed to show you the page and to keep it secure. I do not use this data to identify you or to build a profile of you.

**Contact form.** If you write to me through the form, I receive what you enter: your name, how to reach you (email, phone number, WhatsApp or Telegram) and your message, together with the time it was sent. I use this only to reply to you and, if you wish, to arrange a conversation. I do not use it for advertising or newsletters.

**Other ways of contacting me.** If you write to me by email, Telegram or WhatsApp, the provider of that service processes your message under its own privacy policy. I receive only what you send me.

**Fonts.** The typefaces are stored on this website and delivered together with the page. Your browser does not connect to any font provider.

**Cookies and tracking.** I do not use analytics, advertising tools or tracking cookies. Article pages may remember your choice of a light or dark display in your browser (local storage). This setting stays on your device and is never sent to me.

## Sensitive data

Please do not put health details or other sensitive personal data into the form. Sensitive data includes, for example, information about your health, your psychological state or your private life. If you share such information anyway, you expressly consent to my using it to reply to you. If you prefer, we can talk about sensitive matters in our conversation instead.

## Children and young people

I work with people aged 14 and over. For anyone under 18, a parent or legal guardian must agree to the sessions in writing before we start. If I notice that a message comes from a child under 14, I do not process it further and delete it.

## Who receives your data

- {{form_service}}
- Email providers that carry and store your message and my reply.
- The hosting provider of this website (GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. in the USA), which processes the technical data of your visit.
- Telegram or WhatsApp and their operators, only if you choose to contact me through them.
- Authorities or courts, only if the law requires me to disclose data.

I do not sell your data and do not pass it on for advertising.

## Disclosure abroad

Some of these recipients process data outside Switzerland, in particular in the USA, and, depending on the channel you choose, in other countries. For the USA, the Swiss Federal Council recognises an adequate level of data protection for companies certified under the Swiss–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. If a recipient is not certified or the country does not offer an adequate level of protection, I rely on other safeguards allowed by the FADP, such as standard contractual clauses, or on your consent where the law permits it.

## Your consent

By sending the contact form you agree that I may use your name, contact details and message as described in this notice. You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to me. This does not affect what was done before the withdrawal.

## How long I keep your data

If we do not start working together, I delete your message and contact details no later than 12 months after our last contact. If we do work together, I keep correspondence for as long as it is needed for our work and as long as the law requires, for example for accounting records. Technical data of your visit is kept by the hosting provider under its own rules.

## Your rights

Under the FADP you can ask me whether and which data I hold about you and receive a copy of it. You can ask me to correct inaccurate data, to delete your data or restrict its use, to stop disclosing it to third parties, and to hand over data you gave me in a common electronic format. You can also object to the processing or withdraw your consent at any time. To do so, contact me using the details above. I reply within 30 days. I may ask you to confirm your identity first, and requests are normally free of charge.

You may also contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC, Feldeggweg 1, 3003 Bern, www.edoeb.admin.ch). If European or UK data protection law applies to you, you also have the rights described there and may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

## Security

The website and the contact form are served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Access to my inbox and to the list of requests is protected by passwords and limited to me. No transmission over the internet is completely secure. If a breach of data security is likely to lead to a high risk for you, I will inform you and the FDPIC as the law requires.

## If we work together

This notice covers the website and your first contact. If we start working together, I will tell you separately what notes I keep about our sessions, why, and for how long. Everything you share in sessions stays confidential.

## Changes

I may update this notice, for example when the law or the way this website works changes. The current version is always on this page.
