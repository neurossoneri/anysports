## What data I process, and why

**Contact form.** If you write to me through the form, I receive what you enter: your name, how to reach you (email, Telegram or WhatsApp) and your message. I use this only to reply to you and, if you wish, to arrange a conversation. I do not use it for advertising or newsletters.

**Technical data.** When you open the website, the servers that deliver it process technical data such as your IP address, the date and time, and the pages requested. This is needed to show you the page and to keep it secure.

**Fonts.** The website loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. When a page opens, your browser connects to Google's servers, and Google receives your IP address.

I do not use analytics, advertising tools or tracking cookies.

## Who receives your data

- {{form_service}}
- Email providers that carry and store your message and my reply.
- The hosting provider of this website (GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. in the USA).
- Google (Google Fonts), as described above.

I do not sell your data and do not pass it on for advertising.

## Disclosure abroad

Some of these recipients process data outside Switzerland, in particular in the USA. If a country does not offer a level of data protection that Switzerland recognises as adequate, I rely on the safeguards Swiss law provides for, such as the recipient's certification under the Swiss–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

## Your consent

By sending the contact form you agree that I may use your name, contact details and message as described in this notice. You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to me; this does not affect what was done before.

Please do not put health details or other sensitive personal data into the form. If you do share such information, you expressly consent to my using it to reply to you. If you prefer, we can talk about sensitive matters in our conversation instead.

## How long I keep your data

Messages are kept only as long as needed to reply and to follow up on our conversation. After that they are deleted, unless the law requires me to keep them.

## Your rights

Under the FADP you can ask me for information about the data I hold about you and request that it be corrected or deleted. You can also object to the processing or ask for your data in a common format. To do so, contact me using the details above.

You may also contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC, www.edoeb.admin.ch). If European or UK data protection law applies to you, you also have the rights described there and may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

## Security

The website and the contact form are served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
